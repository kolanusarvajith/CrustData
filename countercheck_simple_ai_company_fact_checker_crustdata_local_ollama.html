What is CounterCheck?

Don't trust what a company claims in an email or pitch deck. Verify it automatically.

Suppose a vendor or startup tells you: “We were founded in 2016 in Zurich, have 450+ employees, and raised $85M.” CounterCheck takes that paragraph, uses your Local Ollama AI to pull out each claim, checks the real numbers in Crustdata's live company & employee database, and shows you clearly what is True (Supported) and what is False (Contradicted).

How Does Local Ollama Work in This Project?

Your local Ollama model (http://localhost:11434) does 2 specific jobs—while strict math rules decide the final truth:

Job 1: Read Messy Text (Ollama)

When you paste a paragraph on Page 2, our backend sends it to POST http://localhost:11434/api/chat with a strict JSON schema. Ollama turns messy sentences into structured claims (e.g. headcount = 3500, founded = 2015).

Middle Step: Fetch & Compare (Crustdata + Math Rules)

Our Python backend queries Crustdata API (using your pre-configured key cd_2fe...ba1c) to get real employee counts, funding, HQ, and founder LinkedIn history. Deterministic Python rules compare the numbers—Ollama is never allowed to guess or fake a verdict!

Job 2: Write the Summary (Ollama)

After the verdicts are locked in, Ollama receives the verified facts and writes the plain-English Executive Summary and Follow-Up Questions on Page 5. Any citation not backed by Crustdata is automatically stripped.
